# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Empathy and kindness
- Respectful disagreement
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing when we get it wrong
- Focusing on what is best for the village and the project

Examples of behaviour that is not allowed:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers via a private channel (GitHub, or the contacts on the [Different Growth](https://github.com/differentgrowth) org). The maintainers will review and respond. They may warn, limit, or ban anyone who violates this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
